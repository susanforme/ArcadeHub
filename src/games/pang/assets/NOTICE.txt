The sprite sheet uses magenta as its transparency key; the Phaser loader
performs that technical color-key conversion at runtime without redrawing
any character, balloon, ladder, harpoon, platform or power-up.

../levels.json is a data-only transcription of src/levels.c stages 1-26,
including original object coordinates, platform types, ladder rungs,
starting ball directions, bounce settings and fixed bonus assignments.

Scope: native Phaser single-player remake of the classic core with four
ball sizes, single/double/glue harpoons, breakable and solid platforms,
ladders, shield, freeze, dynamite and extra-life power-ups, a time limit,
three starting lives, score/extra lives and save/resume.
This is not a ROM emulator or an archival 50-stage port. It includes the
26 consecutive layouts actually present in the chosen community source;
the original arcade release has additional stages. Collision resolution,
continuous-time physics and timing are newly implemented, so the remake
does not claim frame-identical behavior. Two-player mode, enemy animals,
fruit bonuses, the arcade travel map and original music are not included.
