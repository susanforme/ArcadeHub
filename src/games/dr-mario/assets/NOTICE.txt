The sprite sheets reproduce original Dr. Mario characters, viruses, capsules,
and bottle artwork. The miscellaneous sheet credits General Dr. Mario (NES)
Sprite Sheet, Black Squirrel. Dr. Mario and its original artwork/music are
copyright Nintendo. The community repository does not supply a LICENSE file
or an independent permission grant for these Nintendo assets. Public source
availability is not a claim that the original art or music is freely licensed.
The cover embeds cropped regions of the original sprites; only its layout is new.

No upstream JavaScript is bundled. The Phaser scene and TypeScript rules were
implemented for ArcadeHub. Single-player rules follow the original NES manual:
https://www.world-of-nintendo.com/manuals/nes/dr_mario.shtml

Boards are generated from the classic virus-level rules (4–84 viruses at levels
0–20), not hand-authored levels. This is a native recreation, not ROM emulation.
NES random-number sequences, exact frame timings, two-player battles, ending
cutscenes, and the Chill music option are not included. Fever music and shared
ArcadeHub effects obey the platform mute/volume controls and pause with the game.
