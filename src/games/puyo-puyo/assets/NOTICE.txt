puyo-sheet.png is copied unchanged from puyo-ogl.zip. The author supplies
16x16 pixel blobs, with 2px between cells, and frames for connected neighbors.
cover.svg arranges embedded copies of this sheet for the ArcadeHub library.
The independent Phaser scene and rules are implemented in ArcadeHub.
Game audio uses the existing ArcadeHub shared sound effects.

This is community-created artwork for a Puyo Puyo-like game, not a claim of
official game asset provenance. Puyo Puyo and associated original characters
and trademarks belong to their respective owners (SEGA / Compile).
