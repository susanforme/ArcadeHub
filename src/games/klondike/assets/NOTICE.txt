back.png, empty.png and recycle.png were extracted from bitmap resources 54,

The upstream README identifies the art as the original Microsoft Windows
cards.dll artwork. Card faces are credited to Susan Kare; several backs to
Leslie Kooy. Original artwork rights remain with their respective holders.
The community repository does not supply a separate license granting artwork
rights. This notice records provenance; it does not relicense those images.
