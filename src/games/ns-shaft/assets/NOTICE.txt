The upstream teaching repository supplies these classic NS-SHAFT-style
images and sounds without a LICENSE file or individual asset license.
Their presence in a public repository does not establish permission from
the original rights holder. Do not describe these assets as CC0 or original
ArcadeHub artwork. Original NS-SHAFT is by Akihiko Kusanagi / NAGI-P SOFT.
The official page retains copyright and permits distribution of the original
unchanged package; that statement is not a license for extracted artwork:
https://www.nagi-p.com/v1/eng/nsshaft.html

Implementation: new TypeScript/Phaser integration, using the documented
classic rules. Six platform types, life recovery/damage, moving ceiling,
increasing scroll speed, seed-preserving saves and single-player controls.
Unlike an archival port, timing and generated platform sequences are newly
implemented; there is no claim of binary-identical physics or level data.
NS-SHAFT generates its shaft rather than using a fixed authored level pack.
