artwork, not the original Gamos/Windows artwork. Only an accessibility title was added; all visual paths are unchanged. The
upstream project's GPL-2.0-or-later terms are retained with the complete SVG
source and COPYING.
The cover embeds frames from that SVG; its layout was composed for ArcadeHub.

No upstream JavaScript is bundled. The TypeScript rules and Phaser scene are a
new implementation of the classic 9x9, seven-color, five-in-a-row game.

Color Lines was created by Oleg Demin, Gennady Denisov and Igor Ivkin and
published by Gamos in 1992. Names identify the game being recreated.
This game has generated board states, not a fixed collection of level maps.
Sound effects use ArcadeHub's shared audio assets and global volume controls.
